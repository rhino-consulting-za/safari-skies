The game asks for no location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone permission. It has no accounts of its own, no chat and no user-generated content.

Remote settings

At launch the game downloads game-balance settings (prices, timers, ad pacing, events) from this website. That download sends nothing about you beyond what any web request carries (your IP address, seen by the web host, GitHub).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Ads are requested as not child-directed, with a maximum content rating suitable for a general audience.

Your rights (GDPR and POPIA)

You may ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to its processing. Our information officer for the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and contact for the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): Ryno Steyn, Rhino Consulting (Pty) Ltd, morty.ryno@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) or to your local data protection authority.

How to delete your data

  • Your save: in the game, Settings, Reset the game (type RESET): it deletes your camp on the device and replaces your cloud save with a fresh one. Uninstalling the game deletes the device save.
  • Play Games data: in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings you can delete the game's Play Games data, including the cloud save.
  • Analytics: the install ID is random and not linked to your name or account; uninstalling the game ends it, and its events age out after the retention period. To have it deleted sooner, e-mail us and we will explain how to find the ID in the game.
  • Advertising: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Google, Ads).
